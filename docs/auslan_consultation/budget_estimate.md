## Possible funding sources

1. **UQ Graduate School Research Higher Degree Allowance** —
   covers small project costs for HDR students; ~$1,500-3,000 per
   project depending on scheme.
2. **CBCS (Centre for Biodiversity and Conservation Science) at
   UQ** — small grants for student research with a community
   engagement component.
3. **National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) Information,
   Linkages and Capacity Building (ILC) program** — funds projects
   that build inclusion infrastructure for people with disability.
4. **Ian Potter Foundation Education Grants** — supports inclusive
   education innovations.
5. **Deaf Society of NSW research partnership scheme** — direct
   in-kind contribution if the project is co-branded with them.

## Cost-reduction options (if budget is constrained)

- **Reduce Phase B to 8 participants** (4 per region) with a
  caveat in the thesis about statistical limitations: saves ~$525.
- **Combine Phase A consultant and Phase C performer** in one
  person: saves ~$80.
- **Self-record videos at home with consultant's camera:** saves
  ~$650 in studio + videographer; quality is acceptable for a
  pilot prototype.
- **Online validation via Zoom + AUSLAN interpreter:** reduces
  travel and venue costs by ~$500 but requires extra logistics
  and may exclude participants without high-speed internet.

A compressed version of the project (essentially Phase A + an
informal validation with 4-6 participants) is feasible at
**~$1,400-1,700**, which is realistic for a self-funded MPhil
prototype before formal funding is secured.
