# Contacts — Australian Deaf Community & AUSLAN Linguistics

A starting list of organisations and academics relevant to the
participatory validation phase. Verify email addresses and phone
numbers before sending — these are the public-facing contacts as
of 2024 and may have changed.

## Primary — community organisations

### Deaf Society of NSW
- Web: https://deafsocietynsw.org.au
- General: `info@deafsocietynsw.org.au`
- Phone: 02 8833 3600 (TTY available)
- Address: Sydney
- **Why first:** Largest organisation by membership, runs adult
  education programs in AUSLAN, accustomed to research
  collaboration requests.

### Deaf Connect (formerly Deaf Services QLD)
- Web: https://deafconnect.org.au
- General: `info@deafconnect.org.au`
- Phone: 1300 773 633
- Address: Brisbane (close to UQ — good for in-person sessions)
- **Why relevant:** Brisbane-based — geographically aligned with
  UQ. Strong cultural programs.

### Deaf Australia
- Web: https://deafaustralia.org.au
- General: `office@deafaustralia.org.au`
- **Why:** National peak body. Useful for endorsement letters.

### Royal Institute for Deaf and Blind Children (RIDBC, now NextSense)
- Web: https://www.nextsense.org.au
- General: `info@nextsense.org.au`
- **Why:** Has educational AUSLAN linguists on staff and
  experience supporting research projects.

## Primary — academic / linguistic

### Trevor Johnston (Macquarie University)
- Web: https://researchers.mq.edu.au/en/persons/trevor-johnston
- Email: `trevor.johnston@mq.edu.au`
- **Why:** Founder and primary maintainer of Auslan Signbank and
  the AUSLAN Corpus. The single most authoritative academic
  contact for AUSLAN linguistics.

### Adam Schembri (Birmingham, but Australian community ties)
- Web: https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/staff/profiles/elal/schembri-adam.aspx
- **Why:** Co-author of *Australian Sign Language: An
  introduction to sign language linguistics*. Sometimes consults
  on AUSLAN projects in Australia.

### Australian Sign Language Interpreters Association (ASLIA)
- Web: https://aslia.com.au
- **Why:** For interpreter referrals at appropriate rates and
  professional standard.

## Secondary — funding / institutional

### NABS (National Auslan Booking Service)
- Web: https://www.nabs.org.au
- **Why:** Subsidised AUSLAN interpreting for medical and other
  appointments. May not directly fund research interpreting but
  worth a courtesy contact.

### Hearing Australia (research division)
- Web: https://www.hearing.com.au/research
- **Why:** Large research organisation; may co-promote projects
  with an inclusion component.

## How to use this list

1. **Start with one (Deaf Society of NSW or Deaf Connect QLD).**
   Send the email from `email_template.md`. Wait 1-2 weeks.
2. **If positive:** schedule a video call. Bring the
   `sign_descriptions.md` and `validation_protocol.md`.
3. **If they redirect you:** politely ask for the most appropriate
   contact and follow that thread.
4. **In parallel:** send a courtesy email to Trevor Johnston at
   Macquarie. Even a 15-minute conversation with him will
   substantially improve the linguistic credibility of the sign
   descriptions.

## Confidentiality

When recording responses to the validation protocol, **never store
participants' names alongside their ratings**. Use a participant ID
(e.g. `P01`, `P02`...) and keep the name-to-ID mapping in a
separate file with restricted access. This is standard practice
under Australian Privacy Principle 11 (security of personal
information).
