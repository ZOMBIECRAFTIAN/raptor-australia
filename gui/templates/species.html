How the model recognises these species

The CNN was trained on 2,390 images sourced from iNaturalist Australia and the Atlas of Living Australia, using transfer learning over EfficientNetB4 (ImageNet pretrained) and fine-tuned for 30 epochs. Validation accuracy reached 80.8% with an F1-macro of 0.784 across the 8 species shown above.

References: Olsen et al. (1993); Marchant & Higgins (1993); Debus (1998); DAWE (2021); Atlas of Living Australia (2023).
