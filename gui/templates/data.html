How to upload to the Atlas of Living Australia

  1. Sign in (or create a free account) at www.ala.org.au.
  2. Go to My Profile → My Sightings → Submit a sighting list.
  3. Upload the raptor_au_observations_dwc.csv you just downloaded.
  4. ALA's pipeline will validate the records and integrate them into the national biodiversity database (turnaround ~24-72 hours).

The identifiedBy column credits the CNN model explicitly so reviewers can apply appropriate weight to AI-assisted records.
